## Behaviour changes since rc.3

Issue [#10](https://git.cflmy.cn/cflmy/marqdo-cli/issues/10) replaced
`marqdo.analysis` revision 3 **in place**:

- exact `source`/`source_digest`, public document AST, complete heading scopes
  and anchors, entry/manifest/lock/stdlib/extension identities;
- one shared `document_refs` resolver used by Analysis, `check --refs` and
  `inspect --refs`, with `resolved/not_loaded/missing/ambiguous/external/
  outside_scope/invalid`;
- removed the duplicate `commands.rs` resolver and the post-preparation lock
  re-read;
- `--emit` writes owner-only exclusive bytes atomically and refuses inputs,
  directories, symlinks and hard links;
- independent schema + semantic consumers and mutation fixtures; old
  same-revision artifacts are rejected.

Downstream `marqdo-view` and `marqdo-lint` must follow
[`analysis-consumers.md`](../developers/analysis-consumers.md); their tracking
issues are linked from the release announcement.
